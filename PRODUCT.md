## Platform

web

## Users

Balanced dual-track serving two distinct client profiles with equal priority:

1. **High-Growth Startups & Founders**:
   - Situation: Rapid growth phases, launching new products, preparing for funding rounds or go-to-market pushes.
   - Job: Fast turnaround (1–4 weeks) of production-grade MVPs, custom data scrapers/bots, and cinematic launch video reels to validate hypotheses, attract capital, and convert users.

2. **Enterprise Leaders & Engineering Managers**:
   - Situation: Modernizing legacy infrastructure, removing operational bottlenecks, and scaling engineering workflows.
   - Job: Resilient cloud architecture (AWS/Cloudflare/Kubernetes), complex multi-system workflow automation (n8n/Make/Python), and compliance-ready integrations.

## Product Purpose

Tylcao is a hybrid deep-tech engineering and creative media production studio. The product website exists to:
- Establish Tylcao's authoritative dual-pillar positioning across Software Engineering, Custom Automation Scripts, and Video & Motion Graphics.
- Provide interactive proof of capability through the live Tilcayo ASCII terminal mascot, modular service catalog, and tabbed multimedia portfolio.
- Convert prospective clients into structured project intakes via an interactive brief builder with transparent tiers and fast-response SLAs.

Success means qualified inquiries from both startup founders and enterprise decision-makers with clear scope requirements and realistic delivery expectations.

## Positioning

- **Unified Engineering & Creative Studio**: Eliminates the fragmentation of hiring a development agency, an automation freelancer, and a video production house. Tylcao delivers production code, resilient bots/scrapers, and viral/cinematic video under one unified engagement.
- **Mascot & Deep-Tech Identity**: Grounded in the spirit of **Tilcayo** (*Leopardus tilcayo*, the Andean wildcat), presented as an interactive ASCII terminal hero that demonstrates engineering capability right in the viewport.
- **Fixed-Scope Sprint Velocity**: Transparent 1–4 week milestone delivery with defined SLAs rather than opaque, protracted consulting retainers.

## Operating Context

- **Engagement & Commercial Model**: Fixed-scope sprints with clearly defined milestones, deliverables, and SLAs (e.g., 2–5 day script utilities, 1–2 week video reels, 3–6 week SaaS platforms).
- **Intake Flow**: Multi-service project brief builder allowing clients to toggle engineering, scripting, and video requirements, set budget bands, attach briefs, and receive simulated tracking tokens.
- **Client Operations**: Async-first communication, GitHub repos, CI/CD pipelines, containerized VPS/cloud deployments, and real-time operational bots (Telegram/Discord/Slack).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Three Core Pillars**:
  1. *Software Engineering*: Full-stack Next.js/React web platforms, cloud architecture & DevOps (AWS/Cloudflare/K8s), and AI agent workflows (LLMs, RAG, vector search).
  2. *Scripts & Automation*: Python/Bash automation, web scrapers (Playwright/Puppeteer with proxy rotation), workflow bots (Telegram/Discord/Slack), and integration flows (n8n/Make/Zapier).
  3. *Video Editing & Creative Media*: SaaS product launch reels, high-retention short-form content (TikTok/Reels), and programmatic batch video rendering pipelines (FFmpeg/HyperFrames).
- **Constraints**:
  - Strict milestone-based scope boundaries; deliverables are bounded sprints, not open-ended hourly contracts.
  - Terminology distinction: **Tylcao** is the company name; **Tilcayo** is the ASCII cat mascot (*Leopardus tilcayo*).

## Product Principles

1. **Dual-Track Parity**: Treat startup sprints and enterprise infrastructure as equal pillars; never bury enterprise reliability under startup hype, nor hide startup speed behind enterprise bureaucracy.
2. **Technical Depth Meets Creative Polish**: Every deliverable embodies production engineering rigor and thoughtful visual craft — functional code and engaging creative work are never traded off.
3. **Velocity Through Concrete Scopes**: Anchor every offering to fixed 1–4 week sprint timelines and tangible milestones rather than open-ended time-and-materials.
4. **Radical Transparency**: Expose tech stacks, delivery windows, operational uptime, and clear intake parameters upfront; eliminate ambiguity.
